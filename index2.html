How it works

Your phone keeps working, even when you can't.

JobDesk sits on your WhatsApp and your phone line. It handles the back-and-forth and hands you a booked job.

A customer gets in touch

They WhatsApp you, or call while you're busy. A missed call gets an instant text back, so they're not left waiting.

Questions

Good questions, straight answers.

Do I need to be good with technology?

No. Setup happens in a WhatsApp chat, and you can answer by voice note. If you can use WhatsApp, you can use JobDesk.

Do I need a new phone number?

No. You keep your number. Missed calls are forwarded so JobDesk can text the caller back, and WhatsApp runs on a business account we help you set up.

What if the assistant gets something wrong?

You stay in control. JobDesk only offers times, areas and prices you've set, and you can approve every booking before it's confirmed. Once you trust it, you can let it confirm automatically.

I already have a website. Can I keep it?

Yes. We can add JobDesk booking and the WhatsApp button to your existing site, or you can use the website included in your plan.

Is there a contract?

No. Plans are monthly rolling. Upgrade, downgrade or cancel whenever you like.

How long does setup take?

Most businesses are live within 48 hours. You answer a few questions on WhatsApp, approve your website preview, connect your accounts, and you're ready.
